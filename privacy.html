Privacy policy

Noxveil records where you go. Nothing it records leaves your iPhone, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

What is recorded

While recording is on, the app takes your position from iOS, including when the app is in the background or closed. It keeps the time, the coordinates, how accurate the fix was, how fast you were moving, and how much ground that fix counted as seen. From those it works out which 1.9 metre squares of the world you have been within sight of, and on how many separate days.

Where it is kept

In a single file inside the app's own storage on your iPhone. The app contains no networking code at all: there is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software development kit of any kind. Nothing is uploaded, because nothing in the app is capable of uploading.

What the developer receives

Nothing. The developer cannot see your record, cannot count how many people use the app, and cannot tell whether it is running. The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, and that is a description of how the app is built rather than a promise about how it is operated.

The two ways your record can leave the phone

You export it. The instruments can write the whole record out as one file, or as GPX, and hand it to the share sheet. Where it goes then is your choice.

Your iPhone backs it up. If you back your phone up to iCloud or to a computer, the record is part of that backup, under Apple's terms rather than ours. There is a switch in the instruments to leave it out of the backup. It is on by default, because years of recording cannot be reconstructed if a phone is lost.

Deleting it

The instruments have a delete that removes every point, every gap and the whole map, and it cannot be undone. Deleting the app removes the file with it. Neither reaches a backup you have already made.

Permissions

The app asks for location, and explains why before it asks. Choosing "While Using the App" is enough; it still records in the background, with a blue indicator in the status bar. "Always" removes the indicator and changes nothing else. You can withdraw either in iOS Settings at any time, and the app will stop recording and say so on the map.

Precise Location is required. With approximate location the map would be wrong rather than empty, so recording stays off until it is on.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anybody.

Map data

The maps are carried inside the app and are never fetched, so using the app tells no map provider anything. Roads are © OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database License; coastlines are from Natural Earth and are public domain.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a reason to send anything anywhere, this page will say so before that version ships, and the app will say so in front of it.
