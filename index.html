Noxveil

A map where only the places you have actually been are lit. Everywhere else stays dark.

The subject is the range you have not moved in — how narrow one person's world turns out to be. At first the whole world is dark; where you walk, the cover comes off and the map underneath appears. Nothing lights up. The map was always there, and you had not seen it.

The largest figure on the screen is not how much you have lit. It is how much you have not.

Roads are © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License. Coastlines are from Natural Earth, public domain.

The app ships a derived database built from those sources. Under ODbL that derived database is offered under ODbL too, and the scripts that build it from the public sources are in this repository — so anybody can produce the same file rather than take our word for what is in it.
