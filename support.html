Support

The map is black and nothing happens

That is the starting state: the world begins covered. Walk somewhere, and the ground within about twenty metres of where you went uncovers. If nothing uncovers after a walk, check that recording is running — open the instruments from the gauge button on the map. The map says NOT RECORDING at the top whenever it is not.

It says it is not recording

The reason is on the same line. Low battery stops recording at 20 % and starts it again at 30 %. Turning off Precise Location stops it, because a twenty metre radius needs a GPS-grade fix; turning it back on starts it again by itself. A pause you set yourself stays set until you clear it.

Battery

Recording in the background costs more than an app that does not record. Measured over six days of ordinary carrying it came to about one per cent of the phone's daily use, because iOS stops sending updates while the phone is still.

Getting your record out, or rid of it

The instruments can export the whole thing as one file, or as GPX, and can delete every point. Deleting cannot be undone and no copy exists anywhere else unless you made one. Your record is also included in your iPhone's backup; there is a switch to leave it out.
